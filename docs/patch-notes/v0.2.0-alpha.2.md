# NPCortex v0.2.0-alpha.2 — Development Patch Notes

**Date:** 2026-10-21  
**Channel:** `Development` source candidate — **not a stable/public release**  
**Source baseline:** C-S1–C-S4 Persistent Follow Motion Session C + BuildFix1 Option A  
**Previous packaged version:** `v0.2.0-alpha.1` (C2-P1-A World Representation)

## Highlights

This development snapshot takes NPCortex beyond static world scanning to a **physical, player-based NPC that can navigate and follow a player** through bounded, server-verified movement. It brings together the C2 waypoint engine, 3D terrain traversal, Follow Player, safe local steering, and a new bounded Persistent Follow Motion Session designed to reduce needless stop–start movement.

These are **features present in the candidate's source**, not a blanket claim that every gameplay/performance case passes. The packaging session independently ran 17 pure-Java suites and 16 focused backend tests, all passing; Forge compilation and live gameplay were not rerun.

## Added since v0.2.0-alpha.1

- **Navigation:** traversability graph, bounded reliable A*, hierarchical region planner, transactional waypoint execution, and explicit cancellation/reconciliation. Includes dynamic 3D replanning and bounded recovery instead of unverified world edits or infinite retries.
- **Terrain-aware movement:** support/collision-shape inspection and typed `STEP_UP`, `JUMP_UP`, `STEP_DOWN`, `SAFE_DESCEND` logic; graph-checked elevation transitions. These primitives do not mean every terrain fixture is accepted.
- **Follow Player:** target selection by connected player, stop/resume-distance ring, bounded rolling local target planning, Follow journal and `/npcortex c2 follow`, `followstatus`, `unfollow` commands.
- **Player-like locomotion:** optional safe diagonal/turn-aware 3D-path segments, collision-parity/risk-aware block treatment, graph-certified flat shortcuts, local adaptive steering and smooth client rotation presentation; retains Vanilla `ServerPlayer` motion authority.
- **B+ any-angle hybrid:** conservative certified flat corridor plus live swept player-volume collision checks. Local steering may target continuous lookahead within the approved corridor without changing verified route receipts.
- **Persistent Follow C-S1–S4:** C2 can append freshly validated flat WALK continuation to the existing C1 owned motion ticket when loaded ground, token, risk, geometry and limits permit. The owned lease remains bounded (**600 ticks, 64 targets, 96 blocks**, no append in the final 80 ticks), and `/npcortex c2 followstatus` exposes `persistentAppends` / `rollingMisses`.

## Fixes and reliability

- Safer C2 cancel/recovery and transactional reconciliation after interruptions; stale action receipts are not automatically treated as success.
- Player Collision Parity avoids needlessly treating harmless grass/flowers/ferns as solid walls while retaining independent risk avoidance for costly or forbidden blocks.
- Turn-in-place (`forward=0`) no longer performs an unjustified speculative forward collision probe; genuinely moving forward remains fail-closed.
- **BuildFix1:** a minimal two-line fix for an `extended` variable scope error in `C2FollowRuntime.tick` seen in the original Persistent Follow candidate's Windows JDK 17 build. **The patched ZIP has not yet passed a confirmed Windows Forge rebuild.**
